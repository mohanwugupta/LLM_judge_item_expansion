Use meaning, not a single verb or keyword:
- Behavior (5) is a characteristic action of the entity, including flying,
  eating or drinking. Function (24) is its customary purpose for an agent.
  Associated action (21) is what an agent does with or to the entity.
- Outside part (6) is an exterior component, such as a wing or tail. Internal
  part (9) is normally enclosed, such as a heart or seed; exposure does not
  turn it into an exterior part. Properties (7/8/10/11) are non-component
  attributes, distinguished by exterior/interior and visible/nonvisual.
- Material (12) is constituent substance. Physical state (29) is physical
  condition; State of entity (14) is a broader capacity, condition or trait.
  Quantity (13) is count, frequency or intensity, not a bare component noun.
- Contingency (18) expresses dependency or causation. Manner (27) describes
  how an action occurs. Function is not just any possible action or effect.
- Taxonomic relations distinguish broader kind (0), coordinate comparison (1),
  subtype (2) and equivalent name (3). Larger whole (15) is a physical whole,
  not a broader kind. Lexical associate (19) is a word association without an
  explicit semantic relation; Lexical expression (20) is an expression.
- Associated object (32) is a contextually associated nonliving object, such
  as a carrot cooked in a pot. It is not a target component, building (22),
  place (23), nonhuman living thing (26), person (28), or abstract social
  artifact (30). Do not force an associated object into Location.
